# L'Étape Sun Moon Lake — 2026 official fact check

Reviewed: 2026-08-05

## Publishable facts

- The official L'Étape Series calendar lists L'Étape Sun Moon Lake on October
  3, 2026.
- The same official series listing describes a 104 km mountain queen stage
  with 1,500 m of climbing.
- The Sun Moon Lake National Scenic Area independently lists October 3, 2026
  and describes an ASO-certified 104 km challenge route.
- The government event page says the current ASO-licensed Sun Moon Lake event
  began in 2024 and includes a separate 29 km recreational course.

## Identity and source handling

The existing `letape-taiwan` slug is retained for URL stability, but the
current licensed name is L'Étape Sun Moon Lake by Tour de France. A distinct
older L'Étape Taiwan event in Taichung must not be treated as the current race.

The event's English information page contains internally inconsistent legacy
dates. It is not used as the 2026 date authority; the matching ASO series and
Sun Moon Lake government listings control.
