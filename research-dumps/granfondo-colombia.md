# Granfondo Colombia — Research Dump (2026-08-10)

## Identity and eligibility

Granfondo Colombia is an active UCI Gran Fondo World Series qualifier scheduled
for Sunday, November 29, 2026 in Medellín, Antioquia. The organizer's registration
form is live. This is the second edition of the UCI Granfondo Colombia lineage,
relocated after the inaugural December 2025 race in Bogotá.

This identity is distinct from `gfny-colombia`, the defunct GFNY-branded Bogotá /
Zipaquirá event. The completed 2025 UCI event remains in the catalog as the
historical `granfondo-bogota` predecessor; the new Medellín race uses the
`granfondo-colombia` slug.

## Current course facts and official-source conflict

The official sources do not yet agree on the final course:

- The organizer's current route page publishes a 138 km Gran Fondo and a 91 km
  Medio Fondo. Its linked GPX starts in central Medellín near 1,480 m and follows
  a northern high-country loop before returning through Bello. The route page
  says routes may change until race day.
- The UCI Gran Fondo World Series event page publishes a 120 km Gran Fondo for
  all riders, total climbing just below 2,000 m, a 1,480 m start, and a course
  high point of 2,525 m. Its written corridor description differs from the live
  organizer GPX.
- The organizer's regulations, updated May 7, still list provisional 150 km and
  80 km distances and expressly allow distance and climbing to vary by 20 percent.
  The regulations therefore do not resolve the newer 120-versus-138 km conflict.

Disposition: use 138 km as the conservative current training target because it
is the organizer's live route and downloadable Medellín GPX. Use approximately
2,000 m for climbing because that is the current UCI series figure. Preserve the
conflict in public copy and recheck the final event guide before race week.

## Competition format and services

The May regulations describe a mass-start, chip-timed road event with results by
age group and gender. The top three in each official age category and the top 25
percent of each age category can qualify for the 2027 UCI Gran Fondo World
Championships subject to the UCI rules. The event is capped at 5,000 registrations.

The same regulations list:

- 6:00 AM race start from Calle 41 #55-80 in La Candelaria, Medellín;
- USD $150 / $180 / $200 entry-fee phases;
- an official event jersey, finisher medal, course aid, finish food and hydration,
  chip timing, medical support, accident insurance, and mechanical assistance;
- controlled and marked traffic, with some sections fully closed;
- a provisional 6.3-hour Gran Fondo cutoff tied to the older 150 km draft.

The live organizer site links three partner-hotel options and a working 2026
registration form. Final aid-station locations, the updated 138 km cutoff, and
race-week transport instructions are not yet published.
