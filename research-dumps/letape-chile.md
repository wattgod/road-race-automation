# Research Dump: L'Étape Chile by Tour de France

Researched: 2026-07-24. All URLs curl-verified 200 on this date unless noted.

## CORRECTION (2026-07-24, post sol adversarial review)

The original dump/profile claimed L'Etape Chile is the series' "first Southern Hemisphere"
edition. That is FALSE and was caught by adversarial review, then independently verified:
**L'Etape Brasil has run since 2015**, first in Cunha (SP), later Campos do Jordao, then Rio
de Janeiro, and now Serra Negra for 2026 — unambiguously Southern Hemisphere and a full
decade before Chile. Source: https://serranegra.letapeseries.com/about-us (curl-verified 200).
Chile is correctly the series' **first Chilean / first-in-Chile** edition, not a hemispheric
first. All profile language was corrected to say "first L'Etape event in Chile."

The official series history page (https://chile.letapeseries.com/history, curl 200) states
the series spans "25 countries and cities" across "5 continentes" including existing events
in China, Mexico, Brazil, and Dubai — this is the authoritative country-count figure (not
the "20+" figure paraphrased from a BioBioChile headline, which is media secondary coverage).

## Core facts (verified)

- **Event**: L'Étape Chile by Tour de France — inaugural (first-ever) Chile edition, and a
  new country for the L'Étape by Tour de France series (which already includes Brazil,
  active since 2015 — see correction above).
  Source: https://veloracycling.com/events/l-etape-chile-by-tour-de-france-2026 (curl 200),
  https://chile.letapeseries.com/history (curl 200).
- **Date**: Sunday, November 1, 2026.
  Source: https://chile.letapeseries.com/stages (curl 200),
  https://www.ridechile.cl/2026/07/ultima-apertura-de-inscripciones-para-letape-chile-by-tour-de-france-2026/ (curl 200).
- **Location**: Niebla, Valdivia, Los Ríos Region, Chile. Route runs through rivers, forests,
  and coastal (Pacific Ocean) landscapes of the Los Ríos region.
  Source: https://chile.letapeseries.com (curl 200), veloracycling.com (above).
- **Organizer**: World Centric Group (WCG), with the Ilustre Municipalidad de Valdivia hosting.
  Race director quoted: Fernanda Castro ("L'Étape Chile is much more than a race. It's a
  complete experience that replicates the Tour's spirit").
  Source: https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/deportes/mas-deportes/2025/11/08/letape-by-tour-de-france-llega-a-chile-en-2026-cita-esta-en-mas-de-20-paises-y-aterriza-en-valdivia.shtml (curl 200).
- **Roads closed to traffic** for the event; route description emphasizes combining Pacific
  Ocean coastal views with southern Chile's forest/river landscape.
  Source: https://chile.letapeseries.com/stages (curl 200).

## Route/vitals — CORRECTED from official stages page

Official per-route elevation gain does NOT match what was previously in the profile
(60K listed as 600m). Verified directly from https://chile.letapeseries.com/stages (curl 200):

| Route | Distance | Elevation gain |
|---|---|---|
| Family | 30 km | 372 m |
| Short | 60 km | 1,292 m |
| Long (main) | 110 km | 1,804 m |

The 110km/1,804m figure matches the existing profile (already corrected in a prior
"Wave-C" pass per citations note). The 30km family route was previously absent from
`route_options` entirely. The 60km figure of "600m" in the prior profile is wrong —
official is 1,292m.

E-bikes and MTBs are permitted in the 30K and 60K categories (not stated for the 110K).
Source: https://www.ridechile.cl/2026/07/ultima-apertura-de-inscripciones-para-letape-chile-by-tour-de-france-2026/ (curl 200).

## Distinguishing feature (real, non-hype)

Winner of the long-course category earns a spot in the amateur "Étape du Tour de France"
in Europe, held roughly a week before the professional Tour de France — a genuine
wildcard/qualifying mechanism, not marketing fluff.
Source: biobiochile.cl (above, curl 200).

## Series context

L'Étape du Tour de France (the original European event) began in 1993. The series has
expanded internationally; BioBioChile's own headline states "más de 20 países" (more
than 20 countries) as of the Chile announcement (Nov 2025).
Source: biobiochile.cl (above, curl 200), headline URL slug "mas-de-20-paises".

## Field size / demand — projections only, not yet realized

- WCG's own projection: "over 2,000" cyclists and 10,000 visitors expected. This is an
  organizer projection for a debut event, not a confirmed/historical turnout — the race
  has not yet run (scheduled Nov 1, 2026; researched July 24, 2026).
- As of July 24, 2026, WCG opened what it calls the "última tanda de cupos" (final batch
  of available registration spots), with registrants from 15+ countries reported to date.
  This indicates active, growing demand, not evidence of a sold-out or historically large
  field — there is no prior edition to compare against.
Source: ridechile.cl (July 2026 article, above, curl 200).
