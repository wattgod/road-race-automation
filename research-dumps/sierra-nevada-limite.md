# Sierra Nevada Límite Gran Fondo — source record

## 2027 source refresh — 2026-08-13

The organizer and its registration provider now confirm a buildable 2027
edition:

- Saturday 15 May 2027, 08:00 start.
- Start at the municipal sports center in Cenes de la Vega; finish in Plaza de
  Pradollano, Sierra Nevada, around 2,100m altitude.
- 119km and 3,300m of climbing.
- Live Gran Fondo entry is EUR55; a combined Gran Fondo plus Subida al Veleta
  entry is EUR80.
- The event page lists four aid/control points before the finish and a 15:30
  finish closure.
- The late race includes a no-drafting, free-speed timed ascent advertised as
  15km with 1,177m of climbing, from the Maitena restaurant through El Duque
  and La Sabina.
- Bib collection is Friday 17:00-21:00 or Saturday 07:00-07:45, and a start-line
  bag service transfers belongings to Pradollano.

The official course page identifies two crossings of Puerto de Blancares:

- Quéntar side: km37.4-44.7, or 7.3km.
- La Peza side: km79.8-89.2, or 9.4km.

The current organizer pages also contain material defects that must stay
visible rather than be reconciled by inference:

1. The same climb table puts El Duque at km122.1-129.2 and La Sabina at
   km129.5-136.9, both beyond the stated 119km finish. Their names are
   consistent with the current timed-sector description, but those kilometre
   markers are stale and are not used as 2027 route facts.
2. The official GPX download button is labeled as the 2027 SNL route but serves
   `HUEX_GRAN_FONDO_V5.gpx`. The file metadata links to RideWithGPS route
   54649617, its waypoints are Aroche, Encinasola, and Cañaveral de León, and
   its coordinates are in Huelva rather than Granada. It is not usable for
   Sierra Nevada route reconstruction.
3. Two aid/cutoff tables remain on the page. The first lists Embalse Francisco
   Abellán at km37.9 and a 119.3km finish; the second lists La Peza at km32.7
   and a 118.8km finish. The shared later aid points and 15:30 closure can be
   used, but the conflicting first-control and decimal distances should not be
   presented as settled.
4. The regulations page still names 23 May 2026. It is useful for the event's
   general safety format, but not as edition-specific date proof. The current
   Gran Fondo page, homepage, news, and registration provider independently
   identify 15 May 2027.
5. The separate Subida al Veleta is held Sunday 16 May 2027. It is not a route
   option or summit extension of the Gran Fondo.

Scoring refresh:

- distance 4->2: 119km is inside the repo's 80-120km band.
- altitude 5->4: the Gran Fondo finishes around 2,100m at Pradollano; it does
  not finish at the separate 2,500m-plus Veleta summit.
- logistics 4->2: Cenes de la Vega sits beside Granada with broad lodging and
  airport access, though the point-to-point finish requires a return plan.
- field depth 3->2: the published rules provide no age-group categories or
  prizes, and competition centers on a single timed climbing sector.
- climbing remains 4 because the organizer publishes 3,300m total climbing.
- score recalculates from 73 to 64, still Tier 2.

The race is cleared for plan production. The date, total demand, registration,
start/finish, aid framework, and timed-sector demand are current and specific.
Exact turn-by-turn route and climb kilometre claims remain excluded until the
organizer corrects its GPX and stale table rows.
