# Race Around Poland — research notes

## 2027 source clearance — 2026-08-14

The organizer now publishes a complete 2027 event package rather than a projected date:

- Official homepage: https://racearoundpoland.pl/
- Official route framework: https://racearoundpoland.pl/route
- Official race overview: https://racearoundpoland.pl/race
- Official registration: https://racearoundpoland.pl/registration
- Official 2027 regulations: https://racearoundpoland.pl/files/2026-08/regulamin-eng-rap-2027.pdf

The rulebook confirms June 26–July 8, 2027; an approximately 3,600 km paved route
starting and finishing in Warsaw; individual starts at one-to-four-minute intervals;
solo unsupported, solo supported, two-person team, and four-person team categories;
permanent checkpoints; a 12-day solo limit; and an eight-day team limit. The current
site also publishes approximately 33,100 m of climbing, active 2027 registration, and
payment deadlines through May 31, 2027.

The final start/finish location, checkpoint map, technical message, start times, and
start-day GPX remain mutable. Those final organizer materials override the current
route framework and must be rechecked before race week.
