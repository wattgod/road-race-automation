## Current route

Sea Otter Europe's July 2026 announcement and the long-route heading on the
organizer's route page agree on **150 km and 1,756 m of gain** (93.2 miles and
5,761 feet). That value drives the catalog and TrainingPeaks plan.

The route-page introduction still says 2,000 m, so the profile discloses the
conflict. The current 150 km route map is also labelled provisional. Riders
must download the final route during race week.

The long course names four passes:

- Sant Grau d'Ardenya, followed by a forest-to-Mediterranean descent.
- Romanya, on roads used in the second stage of the 2019 Volta a Catalunya.
- La Ganga, on roads used in the third stage of the 2019 Volta a Catalunya.
- Montjuic on the final approach to Girona.

The organizer describes narrow, quiet roads through Monells, but the rules are
explicit that every road is open to traffic. The final sequence climbs
Montjuic, descends, crosses the Ter, and returns to Passeig de la Devesa.

The shorter-route data also conflict:

- Sea Otter Europe's current announcement lists both 70 km and 100 km routes
  at 1,000 m of gain.
- The organizer route page lists 70 km / 800 m and 100 km / 1,150 m in its
  introduction.

Those shorter-route values do not drive the long-course plan, but the conflict
is retained as a race-week recheck.

## Schedule, aid, and controls

- Friday bib pickup: 10:30am-7:15pm at Fontajau Municipal Pavilion.
- Saturday bib pickup: 9:00am-7:00pm on the schedule page. The regulation page
  instead prints 10:00am-2:00pm and 4:00-7:45pm, so the athlete guide must tell
  riders to recheck rather than choose silently.
- Sunday bib pickup: 6:30-7:45am.
- Starts: 150 km at 8:00am, 100 km at 8:10am, 70 km at 8:20am.
- Provisional long-route feeds: km 49 Mirador Punta de Vallpresona, km 74
  Romanya, km 133 Bordils, and km 147 Girona.
- Llagostera control: km 33 at 9:30am.
- Monells control: km 95.4 printed as **11:45 PM**. That is operationally
  incompatible with the surrounding controls and is likely an AM/PM error,
  but it must be confirmed with the organizer rather than silently corrected.
- Campdora control: km 137 at 2:00pm.
- Finish control closes at 2:30pm.

The rules require an approved helmet, visible number plate, and wristband.
Riders must be self-sufficient for spares; the event's mechanical vehicle is a
courtesy and spare parts are the participant's responsibility.

## Price and logistics

The 2026 regulation publishes EUR 49 through May 8, EUR 53 through August 17,
and EUR 60 until registration closes. The organizer-provided day license costs
EUR 10 for riders without an accepted license. Entry includes a custom jersey,
number plate, wristband, welcome pack, provisional route refreshments,
medical support, and courtesy mechanical support.

Fontajau is about 2 km from Girona's train/bus station and city centre. The
organizer lists walking, bike lanes, L3/L4 buses, free unsupervised parking,
and a parking shuttle. Girona-Costa Brava Airport is about 15 km / 20 minutes
away; the organizer estimates about 1 hour 30 minutes from Barcelona-El Prat to
Girona centre by rail. Hotel Carlemany Girona is the official 2026 hotel, and
the festival advises booking early.
