# Imster Radmarathon 2027 — source clearance

Reviewed: 2026-08-15

Status: **CLEARED FOR FULL-7 ROADIE LABS PLAN BUILD**

## Identity and edition

- RC Radsportevents Tirol and the event's official site publish **Sunday, May 23, 2027**.
- The event site identifies this as the **11th Imster Radmarathon**.
- Packet pickup is scheduled for May 22 and race morning on May 23 at Agrarzentrum West in Imst.
- Online registration is open, with advance prices published through the 2027 race date.

## Route A target used by Roadie Labs

- **110 km / 68.4 mi**.
- **2,300 m / 7,546 ft** of climbing.
- **07:00** start from Agrarzentrum West.
- The official course crosses the Mieminger Plateau and Telfs, then climbs Haimingerberg/Sattele before passing Oetz and returning through the Pitztal via Leins, Jerzens, Wenns, and Arzl.
- The organizer describes Haimingerberg as approximately **1,000 vertical metres in 10 kilometres**.
- Published control times are Telfs 09:20, Haimingerberg/Sattele 11:30, and Jerzens 14:30.
- Published Route A support is Haimingerberg/Sattele, Roppen, Jerzens/Gischlewies, and the finish.

## Competition proof

- The official FAQ states that timing uses a **transponder system**.
- The organizer publishes men's and women's age classifications and Route A, Route B, and Panorama results.
- E-bikes are excluded from timing.
- The event is part of the Tiroler Rennradcup framework.
- The roads are **not closed**. Austrian road rules apply, which limits the organization score and must remain explicit in athlete-facing copy.

## Alternative courses

- Route B: 90 km / 1,300 m, with Mieminger Plateau, Inntal, and Pitztal sections.
- Panorama Route: 70 km / 700 m, returning through Roppen and the Imst Gorge.
- Roadie Labs training plans target Route A, the longest and hardest classified course.

## Registration and logistics

- Route A/B entry is €56 through December 31, 2026, €66 through March 31, 2027, €76 through race day, and €80 for race-day entry.
- The Route A/B starter package publishes a shirt, finisher medal, pasta voucher, and sponsor voucher.
- Imst Tourism publishes certified cycling accommodations with secure bike storage, washing facilities, repair support, and cycling information.
- Race packet pickup, start, finish, parking, and the pasta party are based at or near Agrarzentrum West.

## Facts deliberately not claimed

- No 2027 field-size target is published.
- Final 2027 start blocks are not published.
- No claim is made that the roads are closed; the organizer says the opposite.
- The official route page does not publish named climb categories or exact gradients for every ascent.
- Final weather, road conditions, and operational instructions remain race-week facts.

## Rating decision

Roadie Labs score: **59/100, Tier 3**.

The course earns real difficulty credit: 2,300 metres in 110 kilometres, with roughly 1,000 metres concentrated in the Haimingerberg. Tyrolean scenery, transponder timing, useful aid, category results, and an inclusive starter package help. Open roads, regional prestige, an unpublished field size, and normal Alpine destination costs prevent a higher tier.
