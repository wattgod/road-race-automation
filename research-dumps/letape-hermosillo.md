## Current organizer contradictions

The official Mexico serial contradicts itself: it prints **León, Guanajuato**
as the host location, then gives a Parque Madero start address in Hermosillo
and a Bahía de Kino finish address in Sonora. The city-to-coast route concept
is independently supported by the Hermosillo municipality, but entrants need a
corrected rider document before booking travel or packet pickup.

The global L'Étape calendar labels Hermosillo registration **Open**. The
Hermosillo event shell repeats that status, but its registration button still
points to a generic `L'Etape Draft by Tour de France` template rather than a
Hermosillo checkout. The official Mexico serial has a Hermosillo event link,
but the catalog does not claim that an entry is complete until the entrant has
a Hermosillo-specific confirmation.

## Course facts and deliberate unknowns

Municipal and local launch reporting support a mainly flat, fast
Hermosillo-to-Kino concept through exposed Sonoran terrain. L'Étape's own
series page describes its flat events in terms of crosswinds, echelons,
breakaways, and personal-best speed, and says the series is raced on closed
roads. Hermosillo-specific traffic and neutralization rules are not yet
published, so the final rider guide must control.

The following remain unpublished and are not inferred from another L'Étape:

- exact elevation gain and final GPX;
- race start time and wave procedure;
- aid locations and stocked products;
- cutoff and sweep rules;
- entry price and inclusions;
- Hermosillo-specific equipment rules;
- packet pickup; and
- rider/bike transport from the point-to-point finish.
