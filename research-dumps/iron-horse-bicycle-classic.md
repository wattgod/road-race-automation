# Iron Horse Bicycle Classic 2027 source review

Reviewed: 2026-08-15

Status: CLEARED FOR FULL-7 ROADIE LABS PLAN BUILD

## Date basis

The organizer's homepage states that the Iron Horse Bicycle Classic happens every year on Memorial Weekend. The same homepage identifies the competitive Coca-Cola Road Race as the Saturday event. Memorial Day is Monday, May 31 in 2027, so the Saturday of that weekend is May 29, 2027.

This is an annual-rule derivation, not a claim that the organizer has published the final 2027 schedule. Every plan and guide must carry the operational guard below.

## Stable race contract

The current official Coca-Cola Road Race page defines the competitive event as:

- 47 miles point to point from Durango to Silverton;
- category-based starts;
- two mountain passes above 10,000 feet;
- a competitive event distinct from the noncompetitive Citizen Tour;
- four official support stations on the current course;
- current cutoff gates at Coal Bank and Molas passes.

The page and the profile's existing sourced rider intel support approximately 5,700 feet of climbing. The profile previously mixed the 50-mile Citizen Tour distance and rounded 6,000-foot figure into the competitive road-race vitals; this review corrects that identity leak.

## Operational guard

Use Saturday, May 29, 2027 only as the Race Day anchor. The final 2027 schedule, registration, pricing, category waves, rider guide, support plan, cutoff policy, road closure, route file, and weather decision override current operational details.
