# L'Étape Trondheim 2027 — source clearance

Reviewed: 2026-08-13

Status: **CLEARED FOR FULL-7 ROADIE LABS PLAN BUILD**

## Identity and edition

- The official L'Étape Trondheim homepage publishes **Saturday, June 12, 2027**.
- Registration is open for the 130 km and 75 km races.
- This is Norway's official Tour de France-branded L'Étape event and the second edition after the 2026 debut.

## Long-course target used by Roadie Labs

- The organizer markets the route as 130 km and states that its exact measurement is **128 km**.
- The current homepage publishes **1,570 m** of elevation gain.
- The start is at **11:00** from Torvet in central Trondheim.
- The field leaves behind a neutral vehicle; Kilometer Zero follows a 2.2 km controlled rollout.
- Three named categorized climbs are published:
  - Côte de Grøset: 1.9 km at 7.1% (category 3)
  - Côte de Venn: 1.3 km at 7% (category 4)
  - Côte de Ståggån: 1.7 km at 5% (category 4)
- The long route publishes three on-course food stations and returns to Torvet.

## Competition proof

- The organizer explicitly publishes official timing plus overall and age/gender classifications.
- The 130 km and 75 km winners receive Tour de France yellow jerseys.
- Separate Sprint Challenge and KOM Challenge classifications award green and polka-dot jerseys.
- The top three overall men and women on the 130 km route qualify for free entry to L'Étape du Tour.
- The separate 75 km Social Ride is not the training-plan target.

## Support and logistics

- The participant page publishes closed or regulated roads, medical coverage, stationary and mobile mechanical help, food and drink stations, a sweep return to the finish, official results, and a finisher meal and medal.
- Start corrals are assigned by expected average speed rather than route distance.
- Torvet hosts Race Village, start, and finish.
- Trondheim Airport Værnes has train and airport-bus access to the city centre; the organizer also documents rail and coastal-ship options.

## Pricing caveat

- The live registration page showed current Breakaway Bird pricing of NOK 1,290 for 130 km and NOK 1,090 for 75 km at review time.
- The FAQ still displayed an older Early Bird tier. The profile uses the live registration page and labels the amount as current rather than final.
- A Norwegian Cycling Federation annual or one-day license is required in addition to the entry fee.

## Facts deliberately not claimed

- No 2027 cutoff is currently published.
- No 2027 field size is currently published.
- The organizer has not yet issued a final 2027 participant guide.
- The 75 km Social Ride is forthcoming and is not treated as a competitive plan target.

## Rating decision

Roadie Labs score: **60/100, Tier 2**.

The Tour de France operating framework, central-city access, official timing, jersey classifications, and scenic fjord route score well. The moderate 128 km / 1,570 m physical demand, young event history, limited proven field depth, and Norway trip expense keep the race below Tier 1.
